# Private Notes

A small notes web app for individuals on our team.

- People sign up and log in with a username and password.
- A logged-in person can write, list, and delete their own notes. Nobody else can see
  them — not even by guessing URLs.
- Notes are still there when someone logs back in later, even if the service was
  restarted in between.
- One admin should be able to see the list of registered users; regular members must
  not be able to.
